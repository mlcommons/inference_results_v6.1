# MLPerf Inference v6.1 - KRAI

Founded in 2020 in Cambridge, UK ("The Silicon Fen"), KRAI is a purveyor of premium benchmarking and optimization solutions for AI Systems. The KRAI team is proud to be a Founding Member of MLCommons, having participated in over 20 MLPerf rounds to date.

Highlighted in this round are KRAI's participation in the VLM benchmark using `Krai/Gentic`, a novel agentic end-to-end optimization framework.
